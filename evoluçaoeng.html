1950–1960

The first steps

The first electronic games appeared in physics laboratories and universities. Scientists created programs to show off the power of the new computers.

Control console of a UNIVAC, a 1950s computer

1958

Tennis for Two

Created by physicist William Higinbotham in a New York laboratory, it used an oscilloscope as a screen to simulate a tennis match. It is considered the first entertainment video game.

1970–1979

The cartridge era

Fixed circuits, which ran only one game, gave way to microprocessors and removable cartridges. The same console could now run endless different games, a market model that still exists today.

Pong (1972): home and arcade versions
Atari 2600 (1977) and its joystick
Asteroids (1979), as a miniature arcade cabinet

Main games

  • Pong1972 · AtariThe first big commercial phenomenon. It popularized arcades and spawned hundreds of clones.
  • Space Invaders1978 · Taito/MidwayAlien hordes sped up as they were destroyed. It established the High Score.
  • Asteroids1979 · AtariA landmark of vector graphics and Atari's best-selling arcade game.

Main consoles

  • Fairchild Channel F1976The first programmable console with ROM cartridges and a microprocessor.
  • Atari 26001977The decade's biggest icon. It popularized cartridges worldwide.
  • Magnavox Odyssey²1978Had a built-in keyboard and mixed educational and action games.
1980–1989

Japan takes the lead

Games with stories, scrolling screens and levels gained strength, replacing the chase for the High Score. Japan took over creative and commercial leadership of the world market.

Pac-Man (1980) and his ghosts
Tetris title screen
Super Mario Bros. (1985)

Main games

  • Pac-Man1980 · Namco/MidwayThe decade's biggest pop phenomenon. It attracted women players and paved the way for mass licensing.
  • Tetris1984/1989Created by Alexey Pajitnov. It exploded in 1989, sold together with the Game Boy.
  • Super Mario Bros.1985 · NintendoA colorful world that scrolled sideways. It sold over 40 million copies.

Main consoles

  • Atari 52001982Better graphics than the 2600, but it failed due to fragile controllers and no compatibility.
  • NES / FamicomNintendoSaved the industry after the 1983 crash and made the D-Pad the standard.
  • Sega Master SystemSegaNintendo's rival. A hit in Europe and Brazil, distributed by Tectoy.
1990–1999

The three dimensions arrive

2D sprites gave way to real-time 3D polygons, and cartridges lost ground to the CD-ROM, which brought orchestral soundtracks, videos and voice acting. Sony entered the market and shook Nintendo and Sega.

Sonic the Hedgehog (1991)

Main games

  • Sonic the Hedgehog1991 · SegaGave Sega a fast, rebellious face. It made the Mega Drive a hit in the US and Brazil.
  • Doom1993 · id SoftwarePopularized first-person shooters (FPS) and networked Deathmatch.
  • Super Mario 641996 · NintendoTaught the industry how to control a camera in a 3D world.
  • Pokémon Red & Blue1996/1998 · NintendoSet off a craze of collecting and battling. It saved the handheld market.
  • Ocarina of Time1998 · NintendoOne of the best games of all time. It created target lock (Z-Targeting).

Main consoles

  • Super Nintendo (SNES)1990 JP / 1991 USBest-selling console of its generation, with almost 50 million. It had Mode 7 and orchestral sound.
  • Sony PlayStation1994 JP / 1995 USPassed 100 million units. It adopted cheap CD-ROMs and defined 90s pop culture.
  • Nintendo 641996The last major cartridge console. It brought the analog stick and four controller ports: the king of couch multiplayer.
2000–2009

Online and high definition

Online play became popular on consoles and graphics moved to high definition (HD). Motion sensors attracted casual players. Microsoft joined the race and Sega stopped making hardware.

GTA: San Andreas (2004)
God of War (2005)
PlayStation 2 (2000)

Main games

  • GTA III / San Andreas2001/2004 · RockstarGTA III created the modern 3D open world. San Andreas was the best-selling PS2 game.
  • God of War I & II2005/2007 · SonyThe peak of hack and slash on the PS2, with brutal action and Greek mythology.
  • Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare2007 · ActivisionSet the standard for online multiplayer, with levels and perks.

Main consoles

  • PlayStation 22000The best-selling video game console ever, with over 155 million. It was backward compatible and the cheapest DVD player of its time.
  • Xbox 3602005Defined the HD era, popularized Achievements and dominated online multiplayer.
  • Nintendo Wii2006Bet on motion controls and passed 101 million units. It drew in people who had never played before.
2010–2019

Games as a service

Online games turned into platforms, with battle passes and subscriptions like Xbox Game Pass. The hybrid console appeared and big productions reached hyper-realism.

Main games

  • Minecraft2011 · Mojang/MicrosoftOfficially released in 2011. It became the best-selling game of all time.
  • Skyrim2011 · BethesdaA landmark of freedom in RPGs, re-released on almost every platform.
  • Grand Theft Auto V2013 · RockstarThe most profitable entertainment product in history, driven by GTA Online.
  • Fortnite2017 · Epic GamesPopularized Battle Royale and the Battle Pass. It became a stage for live concerts.
  • God of War2018 · Sony Santa MonicaA new take on the franchise, focused on father and son, filmed as a single continuous shot.
  • Red Dead Redemption 22018 · RockstarHyper-realism, AI where every citizen has their own routine and over 200 animal species.

Main consoles

  • PlayStation 42013Winner of the generation, with over 117 million. It invested in story-driven exclusives.
  • Xbox One2013Started badly, with unpopular policies later reversed. It recovered with backward compatibility and Game Pass, in 2017.
  • Nintendo Switch2017A handheld that docks and plays on the TV. It saved Nintendo after the Wii U.
2020–(...)

The current generation

Ultra-fast SSDs almost eliminated loading screens. Ray Tracing and AI upscaling (DLSS and FSR) raised realism, and Cloud Gaming lets you play on your phone or TV without a console.

Main games

  • Marvel's Spider-Man 22023 · PS5/PCThe first in the franchise made only for the new generation, with near-instant switching between Peter and Miles.

Main consoles

  • Xbox Series X2020 · Microsoft12 teraflops and native 4K. It has Quick Resume, which switches between games with no loading.
  • Nintendo Switch 22025 · NintendoKeeps the hybrid format and has passed 23 million units. It is backward compatible with the Switch and runs heavy games like Cyberpunk 2077.
